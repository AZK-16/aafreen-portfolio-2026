Elevating SPONSA’s Brand Identity & Engagement

The Challenge
SPONSA’s landing page needed to better communicate its unique value proposition, build trust with new users, and create a visual experience that reflected its vibrant brand identity.
The Outcome
Redesigned SPONSA’s landing page to create a more cohesive, modern and engaging brand experience, while clearly communicating its multi-goal tipping model to creators and their supporters.
Domain: Content Creation
Time to Results: 3 months
Role: UI/UX Designer & QA Specialist
2 Context

SPONSA is a platform that enables fans to contribute to multiple tipping goals from their favourite creators, offering a more flexible alternative to traditional tipping platforms built around a single goal.

3 Challenge

SPONSA’s landing page should therefore be:

Clear
Help visitors quickly understand what SPONSA does, who it is for, and what makes its tipping model different.
Trustworthy
As a platform handling monetary contributions, the landing page needed to communicate legitimacy and make the experience feel reliable.
On Brand
Create a vibrant and contemporary visual experience that felt relevant to SPONSA’s creator-focused and Gen Z audience.
4 Research

To better understand how users perceived SPONSA, I conducted 7 user interviews alongside my QA work on the platform.

I looked at how users understood the product, what information helped them trust the platform, and where the landing page could communicate its value more effectively.

5 Key Insights
Insight 1
Keep the story simple
Evidence
Users wanted to quickly understand what sets SPONSA apart from other creator platforms, with information that was consistent with the product details outlined in the FAQs.
Design Implication
I explored a simple 3-step narrative (Problem - Solution - Main Use Case) to guide users from discovering a creator to contributing towards their goals.
Insight 2
Strengthen brand alignment
Evidence
One user described the current landing page as looking "alpha-stage in its appearance" and it could be "more fun and less corporate".
Design Implication
I used the redesign as an opportunity to create custom graphics that align with SPONSA's vibrant brand personality.
Insight 3
Emphasise connection with trusted payment providers
Evidence
Users wanted to see a clear connection between SPONSA and its payment providers, with payment information remaining consistent with the details provided in the FAQs.
Design Implication
Showing logos of recognised payment providers such as PayPal as a subtle trust signal could help users feel more confident about contributing.

7 Project Reflection
Constraint
Maintaining Visual Consistency

Create new visual assets for the landing page while ensuring they remained cohesive with the rest of SPONSA’s website.

Constraint
Limited Post-Redesign Testing

Due to project constraints, there was limited opportunity to conduct structured usability testing after the redesign and quantitatively compare conversion against the previous experience.

Improvements
What I’d Do Differently Next Time
Create reusable guidelines for future page designs as more assets were developed
Use quantitative conversion data to better evaluate impact
