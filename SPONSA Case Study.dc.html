Elevating SPONSA’s Brand Identity & Engagement

The Challenge
SPONSA’s landing page needed to better communicate its unique value proposition, build trust with new users, and create a visual experience that reflected its vibrant brand identity.
The Outcome
Redesigned SPONSA’s landing page to create a more cohesive, modern and engaging brand experience, while clearly communicating its multi-goal tipping model to creators and their supporters.
Domain: Content Creation
Time to Results: 3 months
Role: UI/UX Designer & QA Specialist
2 Context

SPONSA is a platform that enables fans to contribute to multiple tipping goals from their favourite creators, offering a more flexible alternative to traditional tipping platforms built around a single goal.

3 Challenge

SPONSA’s landing page should therefore be:

Clear
Help visitors quickly understand what SPONSA does, who it is for, and what makes its tipping model different.
Trustworthy
As a platform handling monetary contributions, the landing page needed to communicate legitimacy and make the experience feel reliable.
On Brand
Create a vibrant and contemporary visual experience that felt relevant to SPONSA’s creator-focused and Gen Z audience.
